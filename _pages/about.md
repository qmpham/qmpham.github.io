I am an Applied AI Scientist at Mistral AI, working on **LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, and agentic systems**. I train and evaluate agents that solve complex tasks through tool use and interaction with their environment. My research interests connect these areas through forecasting, reasoning, and sequential decision-making, including how training dynamics shape model behavior and reliability over long horizons.

My research interests include:

- **Reinforcement learning for LLMs:** curriculum design, reward signals, and training dynamics, with attention to policy divergence, entropy, gradient norms, and their relationship to model performance.
- **On-policy distillation:** transferring reasoning and agent capabilities through training on student-generated trajectories.
- **Deep research and agentic systems:** multi-step reasoning, tool use, information synthesis, and long-horizon task execution.
- **Long-context extension:** enabling models to use longer histories, documents, and trajectories effectively.

I graduated from **[École Polytechnique](https://programmes.polytechnique.edu/cycle-ingenieur-polytechnicien/cycle-ingenieur-polytechnicien)** through the *cursus d’ingénieur polytechnicien*, with a background in **applied mathematics**. I also hold an M.S. in Data Science from Université Paris-Saclay.

I developed my mathematical problem-solving skills through university competitions, including the **[International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC 2012)](https://www.imc-math.org.uk/imc2012/IMC2012FinalResults.pdf)**.
