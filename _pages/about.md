I am an Applied AI Scientist at Mistral AI, working on **LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, and agentic systems**. I train and evaluate agents that solve complex tasks through tool use and interaction with their environment.

My research interests include:

- **Reinforcement learning for LLMs:** policy optimization, reward modeling, credit assignment, and curriculum design, with a focus on training stability and sample efficiency through analysis of importance sampling ratios, KL divergence, policy entropy, and gradient norms.
- **On-policy distillation:** distilling reasoning and tool-use capabilities into smaller, more efficient coding and research agents.
- **Deep research and agentic systems:** multi-step reasoning, tool use, information synthesis, and long-horizon task execution.

I am a graduate of **[École Polytechnique](https://programmes.polytechnique.edu/cycle-ingenieur-polytechnicien/cycle-ingenieur-polytechnicien)**, where I completed the *cursus d’ingénieur polytechnicien* with a foundation in **applied mathematics**. I also hold a Ph.D. in Natural Language Processing and an M.S. in Data Science from Université Paris-Saclay.

I developed my mathematical problem-solving skills through university competitions, including the **[International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC 2012)](https://www.imc-math.org.uk/imc2012/IMC2012FinalResults.pdf)**.
